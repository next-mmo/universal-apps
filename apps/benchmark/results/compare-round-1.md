## Limits

- n=1 per arm. Medians over fewer than three paired runs are not a stable estimate; treat the ordering as provisional until the repeat runs land.
- `totalTokens` is cache-dominated. Quote the fresh `input + output` delta beside it, or quote neither.
- No currency claim: every recovered `cost` object is zero.
- **Provider-cache artifact in this round.** Every arm carries one expected cold turn (the warm-up, before a cache exists), which is excluded here. Beyond it, our re-sent its whole context uncached on 12 further turns, carrying 1,056,895 tokens (86% of its uncached input). That is a caching effect, not code volume the arm wrote. It inflates the affected arm's fresh and total figures, so treat that arm's magnitude — not its rank — as provisional until it is re-run.
- Same machine, same model, same permissions. A different model or a larger change request may reorder the arms.
- R1–R9 are checked by reading the produced source, not by driving the UI.
